About recording and transcriptionRecording laws vary by state. California and several others require every party on a call to be told before it is recorded, and a spoken announcement at the start of the call is what satisfies that. The AI Receptionist can make that announcement on every conversation, and we configure it with you during setup. You control what is recorded and how long transcripts are kept.
